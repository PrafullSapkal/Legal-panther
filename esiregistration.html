ESI Registration

ESI Registration is the process by which an employer registers their business with ESIC to avail the benefits under this scheme.

what is ESI ?

ESI (Employee State Insurance) is a social security and health insurance scheme for employees in India, managed by the Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC).

Importance of ESI Registration

Medical Benefits for Employees: Free Medical Treatment – Employees and their families can avail medical treatment at ESI hospitals. Cash Benefits for Medical Expenses – ESI covers hospitalization and medical expenses for work-related injuries and diseases.

Maternity Benefits: Maternity Leave – Female employees can get 12 weeks of maternity leave with a cash benefit under the ESI scheme.

Sickness & Disability Benefits: Sickness Benefit – Employees are provided cash compensation if they are sick and unable to work, after a waiting period..

Death and Dependents Benefits : Death Benefits – In case of the employee’s death due to a work-related injury or illness, the dependents get compensation.

Document Require For ESI Registration

The documents that are required for ESI Registration-
  • ➡ Certificate of Incorporation / Registration
  • ➡ PAN Card of the Company/Business For Companies/LLPs/Partnerships.
  • ➡ GST Registration Certificate Required if the business is registered under GST.
  • ➡ Proof of Business Address Electricity Bill, Rent Agreement, Property Tax Receipt
The following documents are required:
  • ➡ Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) (if applicable for online registration)
  • ➡ List of Director/Partners (For Company/LLP)
  • ➡ ESI Registration Application Form

Advantages of ESi Registration

Medical Benefits for Employees

Free Medical Treatment – Employees and their dependents (spouse, children, and dependent parents) can avail of free medical treatment at ESI hospitals and dispensaries.

Sickness & Disability Benefits

Sickness Benefits – Employees can avail of cash compensation if they fall sick and are unable to work, after a waiting period of 2 days. Temporary Disability Benefits – If an employee suffers from temporary disability due to a work-related accident, ESI provides compensation.

Legal Compliance for Employers

Legal Obligation – ESI registration ensures compliance with labor laws for businesses employing 10 or more employees (in factories) or 20 or more employees (in other establishments). Avoid Penalties & Fines – Non-compliance with ESI registration can lead to fines, penalties, or legal consequences.

Employee Welfare & Satisfaction

Increases Employee Trust – By offering social security benefits, employers build trust and loyalty with their employees. Employee Retention – Providing health benefits and financial security helps improve employee satisfaction and retention..

Procedure of ESI Registration

Create an Account (If Not Registered)

  • If you are a new user, click on “Sign Up” or “New User”.
  • Fill in your business details like business name, address, and contact information.
  • You'll receive a registration ID and password via email to complete the process.

    • Log in to the Employer Portal

  • Use the user ID and password to log in to the ESIC Portal.
  • Navigate to the ESI Registration section under "Establishment Registration"..

    • Fill in Establishment Details

  • Business Name and Address
  • Type of Establishment (Factory, Shop, Restaurant, etc.)
  • Business Category (Manufacturing, Service, etc.)
  • Date of Establishment
  • Total Number of Employees and their wages (up to ₹21,000/month)

    • Provide Employer and Employee Details Employer’s Information:

  • Aadhaar number, PAN card, and Bank account details of the employer.
    • Mobile number and email address for communication. Employee Details:
  • List of all employees, their names, Aadhaar, and wages.
    • If employees are already registered with ESIC, you can provide their ESI numbers.

    Submit the Registration Application

  • Once all the details are filled in and documents uploaded, review everything carefully.
  • Submit the application for registration.

    • Receive ESIC Registration Number

  • After verification, you will receive your ESI Registration Number (Code).
  • This registration number will be used for all contributions, claims, and communication related to ESIC..

    • Receive ESIC Registration Number

    • Payment of ESI Contributions

  • Employers and employees are required to contribute towards the ESI scheme.
  • The employer's contribution is 3.25% and the employee's contribution is 0.75% of the employee's wages.
  • Contributions must be paid monthly through the ESIC portal.

    • Comply with Regular Returns and Updates

  • Employers must file ESI returns monthly.
  • Regularly update employee details in the ESIC system and ensure contributions are made promptly.
