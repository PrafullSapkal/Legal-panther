RCMC Registration

It is mandatory for Indian exporters to obtain an RCMC to avail benefits under the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP) and to be recognized as an authorized exporter.

What is RCMC?

RCMC (Registration Cum Membership Certificate) is a certificate issued by Export Promotion Councils (EPCs), Commodity Boards, and other authorities under the Ministry of Commerce & Industry, Government of India.

Importance of RCMC Registration

Eligibility for Export Benefits – Required to avail various incentives, subsidies, and duty exemptions under the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP).

Recognition as an Exporter – Serves as proof that the exporter is registered with an authorized Export Promotion Council.

Access to Government Schemes – Enables exporters to participate in government schemes like MEIS, RoDTEP, and duty drawback benefits.

Participation in Trade Fairs & Exhibitions – Many EPCs provide support and sponsorship for international trade fairs.

Document Require For RCMC Registration

The documents that are required for RCMC registration-
  • ➡ Application Form (as per the respective EPC’s format)
  • ➡ Import Export Code (IEC) (issued by DGFT)
  • ➡ GST Registration Certificate
  • ➡ PAN Card of the business
  • ➡ Company Registration Certificate
The following documents are required:
  • ➡ List of Directors / Partners (if applicable)
  • ➡ Details of Export Turnover (if required by the EPC)

Advantages of RCMC Registration

Access to Export Benefits

Required to avail various government incentives, duty exemptions, and subsidies under the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP).

Participation in Trade Fairs & Exhibitions

Enables exporters to join government-sponsored trade fairs, exhibitions, and buyer-seller meets.

Assistance in Policy-Related Matters

Helps exporters stay updated with changing export policies, regulatory requirements, and compliance norms.

Networking Opportunities

Provides access to industry networks, helping businesses connect with buyers and suppliers globally.

Procedure of RCMC Registration

1: Identify the Relevant Export Promotion Council (EPC) Determine the Export Promotion Council (EPC), Commodity Board, or Development Authority that covers your product or service. If exporting multiple products, you can register with FIEO (Federation of Indian Export Organisations).

Submit the Application Apply online through the respective EPC’s website or submit a physical application at their office. Pay the required membership fee (varies based on the EPC).

Verification & Approval The EPC will verify the documents and process the application. If everything is in order, RCMC will be issued within a few days.

Renewal of RCMC The certificate is usually valid for five years and must be renewed before expiry to continue availing benefits.
