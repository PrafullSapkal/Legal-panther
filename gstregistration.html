What is GST Registration ?

GST (Goods and Services Tax) registration is the process by which a business or individual registers under the GST law to legally collect and remit GST on the supply of goods and services. It is mandatory for businesses that exceed the prescribed turnover limit or meet other eligibility criteria set by the government.

Importance of GST Registration

Legal Compliance -GST registration ensures that businesses operate legally and avoid penalties for tax evasion or non-compliance.

Input Tax Credit (ITC) Benefits - Registered businesses can claim ITC on GST paid on purchases, reducing their overall tax liability.

Business Expansion & Credibility - Having a GST number enhances a business's credibility and allows participation in B2B deals and government contracts.

Global Expansion & Market Reach : Facilitates brand expansion into international markets with legal protection.

Document Require For GST Registration

The documents that are required for GST registration-
  • ➡ Copy of PAN Card of Applicnat
  • ➡ Copy Of Adhar Card
  • ➡ Incorporation Certificate or Business Registration Proof
  • ➡ Address Proof (Rent Agreement / Electricity Bill)
  • ➡ Canceled Cheque / Bank Statement .
The following documents are required:
  • ➡Digital Signature
  • ➡ Passport size photograph
  • ➡ Letter of Authorization / Board Resolution for Authorized Signatory

Who Needs GST Registration?

Businesses with Turnover Above the Threshold Limit:

  • ₹40 lakhs for goods (₹20 lakhs for some states)
  • ₹20 lakhs for services (₹10 lakhs for some states)

    • Interstate Suppliers – Businesses involved in selling goods/services across different states.

    E-commerce Operators – Platforms like Amazon, Flipkart, etc.

    Casual Taxable Persons – Individuals or businesses supplying goods/services occasionally.

    Voluntary Registration – Any business can register to avail GST benefits, even if below the turnover limit.

    Procedure of GST Registration

    Step 1. Visit the GST Portal Go to the official GST website: www.gst.gov.in.

    Step 2. Click on ‘Register Now’ Click on ‘Services’ → ‘Registration’ → ‘New Registration’.

    Step 3. Fill in the Part-A Form (Application Form) After submitting, an OTP (One-Time Password) will be sent to your mobile & email for verification.

    Step 4. Receive Temporary Reference Number (TRN) After verification, you will receive a TRN (Temporary Reference Number).

    Step 5. Fill in Part-B Form & Upload Documents

    Step 6. Verification via OTP or DSC After submitting the details, verify using OTP (for proprietors/partnerships) or Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) (for companies & LLPs).

    Step 7. ARN (Application Reference Number) Generation

    Step 8. GST Certificate Issuance If all documents are correct, the GST officer will approve your application.
